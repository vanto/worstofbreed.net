## Analysis
Spec-Driven Development (like the BMAD method) is the natural evolution of Enterprise Agile: taking a simple task and wrapping it in so much process that it requires a dedicated budget to maintain. Instead of just writing the damn code, we now write hyper-detailed, prose-like Markdown specifications that nobody—not even the AI that wrote them—actually wants to read. We have essentially traded deterministic compiler errors for prompt hallucinations and astronomical LLM token bills.

**The Reality:**
We've successfully automated the worst parts of Scrum. A virtual AI Product Manager drafts requirements for a virtual AI Architect, who writes specs for a virtual AI Developer. No actual features go live, but the token consumption is through the roof. The final insult? The agents are programmed to hold a "retrospective" where they pat each other on the back for their seamless collaboration and excellent synergy. It is Executable Jira, but you pay per word.
